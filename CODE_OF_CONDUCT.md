# Code of Conduct

BAR is a security project. Strong disagreement and adversarial review are welcome; personal attacks are not.

## Expected behavior

- challenge ideas, designs and evidence rather than people;
- be specific when reporting a problem and separate observation from inference;
- assume good faith while still verifying security claims;
- respect privacy and never post real credentials, private keys, customer data or proprietary source;
- give contributors room to correct mistakes;
- disclose conflicts of interest when they materially affect a review.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private information, spam, or attempts to pressure maintainers into bypassing security/review controls are not acceptable.

## Security reports

Do not use public discussions or issues for exploitable authority bypasses, credential exposure, sandbox escapes or similar vulnerabilities. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove content and may restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe and constructive.

If a conduct problem cannot be handled safely in public, contact the maintainers through an appropriate private GitHub channel.

The goal is simple: rigorous technical debate, reproducible evidence and respectful collaboration.
